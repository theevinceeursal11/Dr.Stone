About Dr. Stone

In a world turned to stone, science becomes humanity’s only hope. Dr. Stone follows Senku and his friends as they rebuild civilization through invention, discovery, and determination. This is a story of survival, knowledge, and the unbreakable will to push forward against all odds.

  • Senku Ishigami

    The genius scientist determined to rebuild civilization using the power of science.

  • Chrome

    A self-taught sorcerer turned scientist, Chrome is Senku’s curious and loyal partner.

  • Kohaku

    A strong and agile warrior of Ishigami Village who trusts Senku’s leadership.

  • Tsukasa Shishio

    A powerful fighter who believes only the pure should be revived in the new world.

  • Taiju Oki

    A kind-hearted and strong individual known for his endurance and loyalty to his friends.

  • Yuzuriha Ogawa

    A gentle and intelligent girl with artistic skills, supporting the team with her creativity and care.

  • Asagiri Gen

    Gen is a clever talker who always has a plan up his sleeve.

  • Suika

    Small, lively, and always full of energy.

  • Kaseki

    Old, wise, and a master craftsman with a love for creating amazing things.
