About Dr. Stone

In a world turned to stone, science becomes humanity’s only hope. Dr. Stone follows Senku and his friends as they rebuild civilization through invention, discovery, and determination. This is a story of survival, knowledge, and the unbreakable will to push forward against all odds.

  • Senku Ishigami

    The genius scientist determined to rebuild civilization using the power of science.

  • Chrome

    A self-taught sorcerer turned scientist, Chrome is Senku’s curious and loyal partner.

  • Kohaku

    A strong and agile warrior of Ishigami Village who trusts Senku’s leadership.

  • Cell Phone

    Restoring long-distance communication for the Kingdom of Science.

  • Space Suit

    Protects the Kingdom of Science explorers during space missions, enabling survival in the vacuum of space.

  • time-machine

    Allows the Kingdom of Science to explore past and future possibilities, pushing the boundaries of scientific knowledge.
